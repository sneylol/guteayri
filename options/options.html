GuteAyri

Reads the questions on a page, including the images, and fills in the answers using the AI model you choose.

Provider

Your key or sign-in stays in this browser and is only sent to the provider you pick. ChatGPT and CLIProxyAPI run on a subscription you already pay for instead of a key.

Reasoning

How long the model thinks before it answers. Higher levels get hard questions right more often but are slower and cost more. Models without a reasoning setting ignore this.

Instructions

What the model is told before every page. GuteAyri adds the rules for reading fields and returning answers on its own, so you only need to describe how to answer.

Knowledge

Notes the model reads alongside every page: course material, formulas, vocabulary, or anything it should know. Turn a topic off to keep it without sending it.

    Shortcuts

    Click a shortcut, then press the keys you want. Combine Alt, Ctrl or ⌘ with a letter, number or arrow; F1 to F12 work on their own.
